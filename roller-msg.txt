feat(roller): the organ replaces the dice tray — two lines of pills, and the damage half becomes real

The UI half of the roller spec, and with it UBUG-21. The tray used to build the TO-HIT
under a heading that said "Greataxe" while the damage rode along in a queue it could
neither show nor edit — so a "+1d6" typed for a damage rider was summed into the d20 and
the card resolved a quietly-wrong number. The roller has two lines because a d20 test and
damage are different kinds of thing, and the damage line is made of the same editable
pills as the test line.

- `Roller.svelte` + `RollerLine.svelte` — the organ: a header of dice that lands in the
  line the caret is in, lines of pills, and the Roll TAB hanging out of the bottom edge.
  Colour lives in the text, never in a pill's fill; tinted fills are reserved for the two
  state toggles, so "this is a test" and "this line is active" can't read as one signal.
  Typing parses on a space, a letter opens the suggestion menu (a continuation of the
  line, not a dropdown over it), Tab/Enter/click complete, Ctrl+Enter rolls.
- The tray mounts it and hands the completed rolls to the log — a volley logs N lines and
  toasts ONE card, which is the first thing in the app that fires N sub-rolls at all.
- A damage-only roll finally has a card shape: `RollToastModel.tested` drops the empty
  "to hit 0" column a Fireball used to draw.
- The advantage cue's three shapes hoist to `styles/components.css`; the colours stay
  local, because a completed roll wants its neutral diamond colourless and the roller's
  toggle wants all three coloured.
- The damage glyphs move to `components/damage-glyphs.ts`, so the type list has ONE home:
  the icon draws from it and the roller menu offers from it.
- Two silently-wrong numbers fixed on the way: an effect's DICE now reach a prefilled roll
  (alt-clicking under Bless rolled a d4 short of the same roll tapped normally), and the
  reroll/bound facts ride the pool's own dice, so a Great Weapon Fighting reroll can't
  reach a Bless die in the same line.
- `/dev/roller` drives the real organ from a fixture vocabulary; two new shot states.

1420 tests green; lint, stylelint, knip, jscpd, madge and the build clean.
