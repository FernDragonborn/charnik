DC doesn’t scale here — each projectile is under 22 damage, and the DC only rises above 10 at 22+ on a single hit (DC = ½ the hit). So for multi-projectile the DC line is a flat, editable 10; you only change it in the rare case a single projectile was huge.
A single big hit (dragon breath 28 → DC 14) is NOT this button — it’s the normal one-save call-out, where the entered damage does set the DC (variant A).
