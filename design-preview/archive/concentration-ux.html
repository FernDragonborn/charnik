Concentration save — 2 UX variants

Both share the principle: the app never rolls the save for you and never pops a modal on every Damage press (you might be correcting an entry). It only surfaces the option. Scenario: concentrating on Bless, just pressed Damage 22. Fail → the app offers to drop concentration.

A · Smart — highlight + suggested DC

After a Damage press while concentrating, a glowing call-out appears with a pre-filled but editable DC (max 10 / half the damage). One click to roll. Corrections just update the suggestion — no modal.

Hit points
26 / 40
◈ Concentration Bless
Concentration check you took 22 damage
DC d20 + CON save (+3)
Fail → you'll be asked to drop Bless.

B · Minimal — plain always-present button

While concentrating, a plain “Concentration save” button just sits by the indicator. No auto-DC, no highlight — you decide when to roll and know your own DC. Least clever, least in-the-way.

Hit points
26 / 40
◈ Concentration Bless
DC 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher.
Trade-off: never nags, but doesn’t remind you a save is due, and you compute the DC yourself.
