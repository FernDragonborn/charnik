Concentration — multi-save variations (on chosen variant A)

All on top of variant A (surface + suggested DC, player rolls). The question is how to offer several saves at once when RAW wants one per source (2014) or a table rules Magic Missile per-dart. Common case stays one save; multi-save is opt-in.

A · Single save (default — the 95% case)

One Damage press → one save on the amount. No multi-save UI unless asked. This is the baseline; the others only add an opt-in.

◈ Concentration Bless
Concentration check you took 22 damage
DC d20 + CON (+3)

B · Count stepper (×N, quick)

Bump a “sources” counter → rolls N saves at the same shown DC. Fastest, but same DC for each (fine for equal darts; not for arrow + breath).

◈ Concentration Bless
Concentration check Magic Missile · 12 dmg
DC sources 3
→ three separate CON saves vs DC 10.

C · Per-source rows (＋ add source — most faithful)

Each source = a row with its own damage → its own DC, rolled independently. Exactly RAW 2014 (“separate save per source”). Handles differing DCs.

◈ Concentration Bless
Concentration checks 2 sources this turn
dmg → DC 10 14 ✓
dmg → DC 14 9 ✗
Fail on Fire breath → offer to drop Bless.

D · Split total into N (auto-divide)

Enter the total, then “split into N” divides it equally into N instances, each with its own DC. Convenient for Magic Missile: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 each → DC 10 ×3.

◈ Concentration Bless
Concentration check you took 12 damage
split into 3 = 4 · 4 · 4 → DC 10 each
One entry, split when the table rules per-dart; leave at 1 for one save.
