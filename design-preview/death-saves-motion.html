M1 · liquid

Each side is a body of liquid whose surface faces the other side and never lies still; drops pull out of it and are drawn back, and bubbles rise through it toward that surface. Press a wing or a skull to give or take a save — the liquid flows to its new mark. At two failures the block is lit, and the light drifts without ever dimming.

M2 · arrows

Each side is a chevron driven at the other, and chevrons keep marching through it toward the middle. Slowly: it is pressure, not speed. The same presses, the same light.

M3 · the bar as a vessel

The app’s own hit-point bar, grown to hold the fight, with the header the block always has. What fills it is the same liquid: lapping surfaces, bubbles toward the middle, and at two failures the vessel itself carries the drifting light.

M4 · pressure

The slanted fill of T2. The death side leans in slowly, slips back and leans in again, and a shadow reaches ahead of it across the middle. Each failure makes it lean further and sooner. Press the skulls: none, one, two.

M5 · waves

The front’s own edge peels off and travels toward the middle, fading. One failure sends them slowly; two send them close together and further.

M6 · burning edge

The front is a blade of light that wavers and never goes out, and sparks leave it for the middle.

M7 · hatch

Stripes cut at the front’s own lean march through both grounds toward the middle; the death side’s quicken with each failure.

M8 · rays and hellfire

Each side moves as what it is. Rescue is light breaking through cloud: soft shafts that come, thin and go, each on its own pace, spilling a little past the front. Death is a dark ground on fire: tongues rise from its floor, taller with each failure, embers climb out of it, and sparks leave its front for the middle.
